    Privacy: no accounts or logins, no ads, no analytics or tracking. By default your scores and progress are saved only in your own browser and are never sent to us. Save codes are optional: if you create one in Settings, your Pokédex, stats, streaks, records and Versus name and avatar are stored on our save server (Cloudflare) under that random code, so you can load them on another device. We don't store your name, email or any account details with it. Anyone who has your code can load and overwrite that save, so keep it private; you can delete the online save at any time in Settings. Versus mode connects players directly to each other, so others in your room can technically see your IP address. Your Versus name and chosen avatar are saved only on your device; if you host a Public room, its code, your name, avatar and player count are listed on Firebase (Google) until the room closes. The services that host the site and its data (GitHub, PokéAPI, jsDelivr, PeerJS, Firebase, Cloudflare) may log standard request details like your IP address, as any website would.
